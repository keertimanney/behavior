04 / HOW TO READ THIS
One episode.
Different kinds of evidence.
The agent record contains model requests, tools, robot activity, and occasional returned summaries. These summaries are partial records; they are not a complete account of the model’s internal reasoning.
The evaluator record tracks grasp and goal states. It is shown separately because it was not supplied to the agent. A model’s description and an evaluator measurement can support different claims.
The chapters are editorial interpretations linked to specific events. They are not logged subgoals. The viewer includes the full prepared trace and all 108 linked images from this run. A single episode illustrates behavior; it does not establish performance across tasks.
